The app verifies the pinned archive and per-file SHA-256 hashes from manifest.json,
extracts only allow-listed files with bounded output into app-private storage, and
publishes a complete ready tree by same-filesystem rename. Existing ready models
are reused after upgrades without another download. Download staging archives are
removed after installation, failure or cancellation.
